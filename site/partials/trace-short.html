The memory is TRACE

Search that shows its work, and wakes only a small part of the library.

In the sealed library we tested on (812,545 passages, 99,891,846 tokens), TRACE kept 0.9985 of exact meaning-based search’s results on 50 test searches while scoring 0.79% of the rows, measured on one of the library’s four equal parts. That is about 127 times less scoring work, and every answer comes with a checkable record of where it came from.

The planned 100-million-token gate was waived: the library falls short by 108,154 tokens (0.11%). On the same searches, ordinary keyword search found the source passage more often (82% against 52%). These are our own measurements; no outside party has verified them. All the TRACE numbers, the earlier larger tests and the limits.
